How to pray
أَقِمِ الصَّلَاةَ
"Establish prayer."
Surah Al-Ankabut · 29:45
Before you begin
Make wudu
Ritual purification with water
Wash hands · rinse mouth · rinse nose · wash face · wash arms · wipe head · wipe ears · wash feet — each 3×
Face the Qibla
Direction of Mecca
Use a compass or Qibla app. Stand with your entire body facing Mecca — not just your face.
The prayer — step by step
Tap any step to expand ↓
1
Intention (Niyyah)
Make intention in your heart
No verbal recitation is required. Simply intend in your heart which prayer you are performing. Face the Qibla, stand upright.
2
Opening Takbir
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allahu Akbar
Allah is the Greatest
Raise both hands to earlobe level, palms facing forward. This officially begins the prayer — you cannot speak or move freely after this.
3
Opening Supplication (Thana)
سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ…
سُبْحَانَكَ اللَّهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ وَتَبَارَكَ اسْمُكَ وَتَعَالَى جَدُّكَ وَلَا إِلَهَ غَيْرُكَ
Subhanakal-lahumma wa bihamdika wa tabarakasmuka wa ta'ala jadduka wa la ilaha ghayruk
Glory be to You, O Allah, and all praise is for You. Blessed is Your name, exalted is Your majesty, and there is no god but You.
Fold right hand over left on your chest. Recite quietly. Eyes looking at the place of prostration.
4
Surah Al-Fatiha
The Opening — recited in every rakah
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝ اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim · Alhamdu lillahi rabbil-'alamin · Ar-rahmanir-rahim · Maliki yawmid-din · Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in · Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim · Siratal-ladhina an'amta 'alayhim ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin
In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful · All praise is for Allah, Lord of all worlds · The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful · Master of the Day of Judgement · You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help · Guide us to the straight path · The path of those You have blessed, not those who have evoked anger, nor those who are astray
Say "Ameen" softly at the end. Then recite any short surah (e.g. Al-Ikhlas below). This is done in the first 2 rakaat.
5
Additional Surah
Recite after Al-Fatiha (first 2 rakaat)
Surah Al-Ikhlas is the most common for beginners — short and deeply meaningful.
قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ
Qul huwal-lahu ahad · Allahus-samad · Lam yalid wa lam yulad · Wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahad
Say: He is Allah, the One · Allah, the Eternal Refuge · He neither begets nor was born · Nor is there to Him any equivalent
6
Bowing (Ruku)
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْعَظِيمِ
Subhana rabbiyal-'azim
Glory be to my Lord, the Most Great
Say Allahu Akbar as you bow. Back parallel to ground, hands gripping knees. Recite 3 times minimum.
7
Rising from Ruku
سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ
سَمِعَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ حَمِدَهُ ۝ رَبَّنَا وَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ
Sami'allahu liman hamidah · Rabbana wa lakal-hamd
Allah hears whoever praises Him · Our Lord, all praise is Yours
Say "Sami'allahu liman hamidah" as you rise. Once fully upright, say "Rabbana wa lakal-hamd." Stand briefly in stillness.
8
First Prostration (Sujud)
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
Subhana rabbiyal-a'la
Glory be to my Lord, the Most High
7 body parts must touch the ground: forehead + nose, both palms, both knees, both toes. Fingers pointing toward Qibla. Recite 3 times.
9
Sitting between Prostrations
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي
Rabbigh-firli
O my Lord, forgive me
Rise from sujud saying Allahu Akbar. Sit briefly on your left foot. Recite once, then go into the second prostration.
10
Second Prostration (Sujud)
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
سُبْحَانَ رَبِّيَ الْأَعْلَى
Subhana rabbiyal-a'la
Glory be to my Lord, the Most High
Same as the first sujud. Recite 3 times. This completes one full rakah (unit of prayer).
11
Tashahhud (sitting)
التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ…
التَّحِيَّاتُ لِلَّهِ وَالصَّلَوَاتُ وَالطَّيِّبَاتُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ أَيُّهَا النَّبِيُّ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتُهُ، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْنَا وَعَلَى عِبَادِ اللَّهِ الصَّالِحِينَ، أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ
At-tahiyyatu lillahi was-salawatu wat-tayyibat · As-salamu 'alayka ayyuhan-nabiyyu wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh · As-salamu 'alayna wa 'ala 'ibadil-lahis-salihin · Ashhadu an la ilaha illallahu wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh
All greetings, prayers and good things are for Allah · Peace be upon you, O Prophet, and the mercy and blessings of Allah · Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah · I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that Muhammad is His servant and messenger
Sit after the 2nd rakah and the final rakah. Raise your right index finger slightly when saying "ashhadu".
12
Salawat (final sitting only)
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ…
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ
Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa 'ala ali Muhammad, kama sallayta 'ala Ibrahima wa 'ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidun Majid
O Allah, send blessings upon Muhammad and his family, as You sent blessings upon Ibrahim and his family. Indeed You are Praiseworthy and Glorious.
Recited only in the final sitting, after Tashahhud.
13
Ending — Tasleem
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ وَرَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ
As-salamu 'alaykum wa rahmatullah
Peace be upon you and the mercy of Allah
Turn your head to the right and say it, then turn to the left and repeat. The prayer is complete. 🤲

Al-Shi'r al-Sufi
Sufi poetry
R
Jalal ad-Din Rumi
1207 – 1273 · Persia
بشنو این نی چون شکایت می‌کند از جدایی‌ها حکایت می‌کند
Listen to the reed, how it tells a tale of separations,
crying of the pain of longing.
The reed flute weeps because it has been cut from the reed bed — Rumi's metaphor for the soul's longing to return to its origin in God. This is the heart of Sufi longing, the ache of love for the Divine.
Ra
Rabia al-Adawiyya
717 – 801 · Basra
أُحِبُّكَ حُبَّيْنِ حُبَّ الهَوَى وَحُبًّا لِأَنَّكَ أَهْلٌ لِذَاكَ
I love You with two loves — a love of passion
and a love because You are worthy of love.
Rabia distinguished between loving God for what He gives us, and loving God simply because He is God. The second love, she said, is the purest — to love without any reason except that He deserves all love.
H
Hafiz of Shiraz
1315 – 1390 · Persia
هر که را اسرار حق آموختند مهر کردند و دهانش دوختند
Those who are taught the secrets of God —
their lips are sealed, their mouths stitched shut.
Hafiz speaks of the paradox at the heart of mystical knowledge — the closer one comes to God, the more one realizes that words cannot carry the experience. True knowing becomes a beautiful silence.

Al-Tahara
Wudu — Purification
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا قُمْتُمْ إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ فَاغْسِلُوا وُجُوهَكُمْ
"O believers! When you rise for prayer, wash your faces…"
Surah Al-Ma'idah · 5:6
10 steps of wudu
1
Intention (Niyyah)
نِيَّة
Make the intention in your heart to purify yourself for prayer. No need to say it aloud.
2
Say Bismillah
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Say "Bismillah" before beginning. Bismillah — In the name of Allah.
3
Wash Hands
Wash both hands up to the wrists, making sure water reaches between the fingers.
× 3 times
4
Rinse Mouth
Take water into your mouth, swish it around thoroughly, and spit it out.
× 3 times
5
Rinse Nose
Sniff water gently into your nostrils and blow it out. Use the left hand to blow.
× 3 times
6
Wash Face
Wash your entire face — from hairline to chin, and from ear to ear. No dry spots.
× 3 times
7
Wash Arms
Wash your right arm from fingertips to elbow, then left arm. Include the elbow.
× 3 times each
8
Wipe Head (Masah)
Wet your hands and wipe over your head — from front to back with both hands, then back to front.
× 1 time
9
Wipe Ears
With the same wet hands: index fingers inside the ears, thumbs behind the earlobes.
× 1 time
10
Wash Feet
Wash your right foot up to the ankle, including between toes. Then the left foot.
× 3 times each
